Engineered protein therapeutics for metabolic disease

Synceptor, Inc. is a preclinical-stage biotechnology company in San Diego. We design and develop engineered protein therapeutics for obesity, type 2 diabetes and metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH).

Our approach

Our name describes how we design medicines: synergistic, synthetic and synchronous receptor targeting. We engineer protein therapeutics that act on more than one metabolic hormone pathway from a single molecule, using receptor pharmacology, protein engineering and computational design.

Synergistic

Engineered ligands with a balanced, selective receptor activation profile, keeping metabolic benefits while limiting signaling linked to unwanted effects.

Synthetic

Designed molecules that combine the activities of complementary metabolic hormone pathways, a pairing not found together in any single natural hormone.

Synchronous

Both activities are delivered by a single molecule, engaging the two pathways at the same time and in the same tissues for a coordinated metabolic response.

Focus

We are developing therapeutics for obesity, type 2 diabetes and MASH. Our lead program is a first-in-class therapeutic directed at a novel target, and our molecules are designed for a differentiated therapeutic profile.

Target therapeutic profile

Balanced weight loss

Combines reduced food intake with increased energy expenditure, rather than relying on appetite suppression alone.

Lean mass preservation

Weight loss driven predominantly by loss of fat, with lean mass preserved on body composition imaging.

Durable weight loss

Limited weight regain in nonhuman primates, consistent with a potential first-in-class mechanism acting on the central melanocortin pathway that governs long-term energy balance.

Gastrointestinal tolerability

Minimal delay in gastric emptying and low taste aversion in preclinical models compared with the GLP-1 receptor agonist class.

Long-acting pharmacology

Extended half-life in nonhuman primates, supporting potentially infrequent dosing.

Glycemic control

Robust glucose lowering and improved insulin sensitivity.

Liver fat and lipids

Reductions in liver fat and circulating cholesterol, relevant to MASH and cardiometabolic risk.

Profile based on preclinical studies in rodents and nonhuman primates. Our therapeutics have not been evaluated in humans.
