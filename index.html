Engineered protein therapeutics for metabolic disease

Synceptor, Inc. is a preclinical-stage biotechnology company in San Diego. We design and develop engineered protein therapeutics for obesity, type 2 diabetes and metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH).

Focus

Our programs combine receptor pharmacology, protein engineering and computational design to build biologics that act on the signaling pathways controlling energy balance, glucose homeostasis and liver lipid metabolism. Our lead program is a first-in-class therapeutic directed at a novel target.

Obesity

Therapeutics that may reduce body weight while preserving lean mass and metabolic health.

Type 2 diabetes

Approaches that target insulin sensitivity and glycemic control.

MASH

Biologics aimed at hepatic steatosis, inflammation and fibrosis.

Team

Synceptor's founding team brings more than 75 years of combined research experience in metabolic disease, with scientific training at Harvard, UC Berkeley and the Salk Institute. The team has a track record in biologics discovery and development, including advancing multiple programs from concept into clinical trials.
