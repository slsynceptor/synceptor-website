Engineered protein therapeutics for metabolic disease

Synceptor, Inc. is a preclinical-stage biotechnology company in San Diego. We design and develop engineered protein therapeutics for obesity, type 2 diabetes and metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH).

Focus

Our programs combine receptor pharmacology, protein engineering and computational design to build biologics that act on the signaling pathways controlling energy balance, glucose homeostasis and liver lipid metabolism.

Obesity

Therapeutics that may reduce body weight while preserving lean mass and metabolic health.

Type 2 diabetes

Approaches that target insulin sensitivity and glycemic control.

MASH

Biologics aimed at hepatic steatosis, inflammation and fibrosis.

Leadership

Sihao Liu, Ph.D.

Co-founder and Chief Scientific Officer

Sihao Liu has worked on the biology of metabolic disease for more than a decade. He trained as a postdoctoral fellow in the laboratory of Ronald M. Evans at the Salk Institute for Biological Studies and later served there as a Project Scientist, studying hormonal signaling in metabolism.

He was a Senior Scientist at Ionis Pharmaceuticals on an obesity drug discovery program and co-founded Pontem Therapeutics before co-founding Synceptor.
